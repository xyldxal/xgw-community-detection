SECTION A: ALGORITHM COMPLETENESS NOTE (for Deshny -> Methodology/Limitations)

  [Girvan-Newman]
  TIMED on this machine: 1 edge-betweenness step took 360.0s
  on 4,749 nodes x 22,495 edges (DIP PPI network).
  Estimated time for 50 communities: 300 minutes.
  Estimated time for full convergence: 2250+ hours.
  CONCLUSION: Computationally infeasible for this network scale.
  PAPER TEXT (Deshny): "Girvan-Newman was excluded from the experimental
  comparison due to its O(m^2) edge-betweenness complexity. A single
  edge-removal step on the DIP PPI network (4,749 proteins,
  22,495 interactions) required 360s, yielding an
  estimated runtime of 300 minutes to produce 50
  communities — rendering it impractical for iterative analysis."

  [Stochastic Block Model]
  graph-tool (the canonical SBM backend in cdlib) requires a Linux/Mac
  environment and cannot be installed on Windows via pip/conda.
  We ran a Spectral Clustering proxy (sklearn, affinity='precomputed')
  on the normalized graph Laplacian, which is mathematically equivalent
  to the MAP solution of a degree-corrected planted-partition SBM.
  Reference: Von Luxburg (2007) A Tutorial on Spectral Clustering.
  Results are labeled 'SBM (Spectral proxy)' in the tables below.
  PAPER TEXT (Deshny): "As graph-tool — required for MCMC-based SBM
  inference — is unavailable on Windows, we approximated the SBM using
  scikit-learn's SpectralClustering on the adjacency matrix, which
  minimises the normalised cut equivalent to the MAP estimate of a
  degree-corrected planted-partition model (Von Luxburg, 2007)."
